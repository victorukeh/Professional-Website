## How This Rate Card Is Built

Every offer here is designed around Hormozi's **Value Equation**:

> **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Success) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**

So each offer does four things:

1. **Dream Outcome:** it names the result the client actually wants, like a live product or customers paying. "Backend development" alone isn't the result.
2. **Likelihood of Success:** it raises their confidence through proof, a clear process and guarantees.
3. **Time Delay:** it shortens the wait with fixed timelines, fast starts and quick first wins.
4. **Effort & Sacrifice:** it takes work off their plate. You handle setup, hosting, documentation and handover.

Each offer follows the same structure: **Name → Who it's for → Dream outcome → Problems solved → What's included → Bonuses → Guarantee → Scarcity/urgency → Price.**

Offer names follow Hormozi's **MAGIC** naming formula: a **M**agnetic reason, the **A**vatar it's for, the **G**oal, an **I**ndicated time frame, and a **C**ontainer word (sprint, package, program, plan).
