Terms

How engagements work

  • Payment: small tasks are 50–100% upfront; projects run on milestones (e.g. 40% / 30% / 30%).
  • Not included: hosting, domains, and third-party API fees (SMS, KYC, payment gateway charges) are paid by the client.
  • Scope changes: priced separately via a change request.
  • Rush delivery: +25–50%.
  • Long projects (3+ months): prices may be adjusted for major exchange-rate changes affecting dollar-based costs.
  • Prices shown are starting points. Final quote depends on scope, confirmed after the Blueprint Session.
